miApps

A collection of local-first Progressive Web Applications by Mitchell. Pronounced "my", spelled "mi". Build without boundaries, right in your browser.

📝 miNotes

A serverless, local-first Markdown note-taking app. Your notes live as plain .md files on your own disk — no backend, no account, fully offline.

Open App

💼 miWork

A comprehensive Kanban-style task tracker. Break down tasks, track progress, and view metrics with a visually pleasing interface.

Open App

🏋️ miFitness

A local-first strength training log. Routines, rest timers, personal records and progress charts — all stored in your browser, fully offline.
