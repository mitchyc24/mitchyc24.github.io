Joint Command

Two commanders, one operation. One of you fights 1 Canadian Mechanized Brigade Group on the ground; the other runs the air task force from the CAOC. Neither of you can take the objectives alone.

Host

You pick your command and open a room. Your partner joins with the code.

Link without a relay

Create an invite code, send it to your partner over Discord, and paste the reply code they send back.

Resume a saved operation

Load a file made with Save, then open a room or start solo.

Join

Enter the room code your partner reads out. You take the other command.

Link without a relay

Paste the invite code from the host, then send the reply code back.

Solo

Command both components yourself and switch desks whenever you like. Good for learning the tools before you link up.

Linked

The link is direct, browser to browser. A public relay is only used to swap connection details, encrypted with a key made from your room code.

How to play

You are a commander in a headquarters. You give orders and taskings; units carry them out on their own and report back. The clock starts stopped so you can plan — either of you can start and stop it.

Army desk

  • On the map, moving dashes show who is firing on whom: blue is ours, red is theirs, an arc is artillery, and a red ring marks a unit under fire from an enemy nobody has located. Dotted lines show where units are headed (blue to move, red to attack).
  • Drag the top edge of the signals log, or use its arrow buttons, to make it taller or shorter.
  • Click a unit on the map or in the order of battle. Click a battalion name to select all of it. Shift-click adds; Shift + drag boxes a group.
  • Right-click the ground to move. Right-click an enemy, or Ctrl + right-click the ground, to attack. Attacking units stop and fight when the enemy is in range. Shift + right-click adds a waypoint: units visit up to eight in order (on touch, use Add waypoint). Hover over an order button, or press “What do these do?”, for what each order means. With a unit selected, the corner of the map shows the distance and driving time to wherever you point.
  • Artillery supports on its own once it has been stationary for five minutes. Select an enemy to give the batteries a priority fire mission. To shell a fixed position, select a battery and Ctrl + right-click the ground (or use its Fire mission button): it stays put and fires on that grid for 15 minutes, whether or not anything has been spotted there. Unobserved fire does half damage.
  • Transport platoons resupply on their own from the brigade support area. Move the support area forward as you advance.
  • You have no aircraft. Ask the CAOC: close air support, strike, ISR, combat air patrol, medevac, air resupply, airlift for 3 PPCLI. Units send their own medevac request once they have eight casualties waiting.
  • Close air support is far more effective when a company with a JTAC is within 5 km of the target.

Air desk

  • Requests from the brigade arrive in the queue on the right. Assign a squadron, retask a flight already airborne, or reply unable.
  • Or task on your own: pick a squadron, a mission, the number of aircraft, then click the map. Turn on “Relieve on station” for a patrol, ISR orbit or tanker and the squadron keeps it covered without you.
  • Aircraft need turnaround time after landing. Fuel and precision munitions at Kestrel Field are finite and are topped up at 0600.
  • Radar SAM sites cover a 22 km ring. Flights route around rings you know about. Find them with the Aurora, then kill them with a strike or with the brigade's guns.
  • Canada has no ground-based air defence here. Only a combat air patrol stops enemy air raids.
  • A tanker on station doubles fighter time on station. Helicopters do not fly in low cloud and rain.

Keys

  • Space stop or start the clock
  • 1–4 time compression
  • M move, A attack, D go firm
  • Esc cancel, F whole map
  • Drag to pan, wheel to zoom
  • Shift + drag selects every unit in the box
  • Alt + click drops a marker your partner sees

Touch

  • Drag to pan, pinch to zoom, tap to select.
  • Tap Move or Attack, then tap the map. Or press and hold the map: the selected units move there, or attack if you hold on an enemy.
  • The Box and Mark buttons on the map stand in for Shift + drag and Alt + click.
  • On a phone the panels are tabs under the map. Tap the open tab again to fold it away.

Clock

With Auto-slow on, the clock drops to 20× when a unit comes into contact, an immediate request arrives, an aircraft is lost or an objective changes hands, so you can run fast through the quiet hours.

Winning

Hold Dunmere, Stannick and Vossberg before D+2 0500. You are graded on what it cost: soldiers killed, wounded who were not evacuated in time, and aircraft lost.
