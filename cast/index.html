Nothing found — narrow it down

Your app is unpublished, so a Chromecast only offers it once that exact serial number is registered and the device has been rebooted afterwards. Google's Default Media Receiver is published and available on every Chromecast, so trying it separates the two causes:

Test discovery with Google's default receiver

If the default receiver does find your TV
Discovery and your network are fine. It is app availability: re-check the serial number in the console, wait the full 15 minutes, then pull the Chromecast's power for 10 seconds and plug it back in.
If it finds nothing either
The problem is discovery, not this app. In rough order of likelihood: a VPN or corporate network client on this PC (mDNS does not survive one); the Chromecast on a different SSID, guest network or VLAN; AP isolation on the router; the device asleep or unplugged; mDNS blocked by Windows Firewall. Also check the clock — more than ten minutes of skew between PC and Chromecast makes Cast authentication fail outright.

Quickest independent check: Chrome's own menu → Cast. That does generic discovery with no app ID involved. If your Ultra is missing there too, nothing in this project can help until the network is fixed.
